PT-BR Audio Transcription & IPA Assessment

Orthographic and IPA transcription with phonetic notes — marking native reductions, regional rhotics and nasal vowels, and separating real deviations from legitimate variation.

ORTH "Bom, eu tava pensando em ir na reunião mais cedo…"
IPA [ˈbõj̃, ew ˈtavɐ pẽˈsɐ̃du ĩj̃ iɾ nɐ ʁeũˈnjɐ̃w̃ majs ˈsedu…]
NOTE ˈtavɐ — colloquial reduction of estava; native marker, not an error.
NOTE [ʁ] — São Paulo rhotic; consistent with the target variety.
NOTE [ɐ̃] — nasal vowel realized naturally; no substitution affecting meaning.
Problem

Reliable speech evaluation depends on accurate transcription and phonetic annotation — without them, nativeness judgments become impressions.

Solution

Orthographic + IPA annotation, marking native reductions, regional rhotics and nasal vowels, with short English notes distinguishing native features from real deviations.

Outcome

Transcription + IPA evidence supporting consistent scoring of pronunciation and nativeness — same protocol used for NURC-SP annotation.
